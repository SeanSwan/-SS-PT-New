### ANSWERS TO CEO QUESTIONS

**1. QR Code Library: `qrcode.react`**
Use `qrcode.react`. It renders as an SVG (crisp on all 10 breakpoints, especially Retina/4K displays) and allows us to embed a logo in the center of the QR code. It is the industry standard for React. Do not use canvas-based renderers; they blur on high-DPI screens.

**2. Claim Route: Standalone `/claim/:token`**
It must be a standalone route. Routing to `/signup?claim=TOKEN` dumps a VIP client directly into a generic form. 
*UX Flow:* `/claim/:token` acts as a "Welcome Vault." They see a beautifully animated Crystalline Swan landing page: *"You've been invited by [Trainer Name] to SwanStudios."* with a Cosmic Nebula CTA (`#8B5CF6` to `#60C0F0`) saying "Activate My Profile". Clicking this *then* pushes them to the signup flow with their context pre-loaded.

**3. Move Fitness Logo: Transparent PNG/SVG REQUIRED**
**Do not use CSS hacks.** Trying to use `mix-blend-mode: screen` or `color-burn` to hide a black background against our Carbon (`#141419`) or Graphite (`#1A1A24`) panels will result in a muddy, amateur "AI slop" halo effect. 
*Action:* You (CEO) must generate or extract a clean, transparent-background PNG or SVG of `MoveFitLogo-clean`. Quality cannot be compromised here.

**4. AI Assistant Token Gen: Auto-Generate, Manual Handoff**
When the AI creates a client, it should *automatically generate* the token in the database, but it should *not* auto-email or auto-SMS the client. The AI should reply: *"Client Anand Patel created. Here is their secure invite code: SWAN-A7X3."* and render the QR/Copy UI directly in the chat stream. The trainer retains the power of the handoff.
