*   **Visual Hierarchy of Tiers:** The plan lacks a visual distinction strategy for the three tiers. If they look like standard SaaS pricing columns, we fail. They must feel like ascending levels of a luxury vault.
*   **Donation Slider UX:** A standard HTML range input is unacceptable. The Guardian tier donation slider needs custom touch-target padding (44px min), custom track filling, and a glowing thumb.
