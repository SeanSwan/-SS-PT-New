### 2. ANSWER TO QUESTION 6: OVERVIEW TAB DESIGN STRATEGY

**My Design Authority Verdict: BENTO-BOX GRID.**

A vertical scroll feed feels like a generic social app or a basic SaaS dashboard. SwanStudios is a premium, NASM-certified platform. The Overview tab is the user's **Command Center**. It needs to feel like a high-end competitive arena mixed with a luxury vault.

A Bento-box grid allows the user to absorb their macro-status (Readiness, Next Protocol, AI Insights) in a single, dense, highly-glanceable viewport without scrolling. Best-in-class apps like Apple Fitness+ utilize this asymmetric density to establish hierarchy.

#### The Crystalline Swan Bento Architecture (Desktop 1024px+)
We will use a 12-column CSS Grid.

1.  **The Hero Cell (Spans 8 cols, 2 rows): "The AI Protocol"**
    *   **Visuals:** `Graphite #1A1A24` background. Top-left features a `Cormorant Garamond` italicized greeting ("*Good Morning, Athlete*").
    *   **Content:** The immediate next workout or daily AI synthesis.
    *   **CTA:** A Midnight Sapphire `#002060` button. Per our Dual-Button rules, on hover, it emits a Wing Purple `#8B5CF6` glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 15px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.5);`

2.  **The Biometric Snapshot (Spans 4 cols, 1 row): "Readiness Score"**
    *   **Visuals:** `Carbon #141419` background.
    *   **Data:** Large `Sora` typography for the score (e.g., "92").
    *   **Accent:** An SVG circular progress ring using `Ice Wing #60C0F0` for the stroke, with a subtle `drop-shadow(0 0 4px rgba(96, 192, 240, 0.6))` to give it that frozen enchanted glow.

3.  **The Arena Status (Spans 4 cols, 1 row): "Weekly XP / Streak"**
    *   **Visuals:** `Carbon #141419` background.
    *   **Accent:** If the user is on a streak, we introduce our luxury accent, `Gilded Fern #C6A84B`, for the streak flame/icon to signify rare achievement.

4.  **The Cold Metrics (Spans 12 cols, 1 row): "Weekly Volume Chart"**
    *   **Visuals:** A wide, short cell. `Carbon #141419` background.
    *   **Data:** Bar chart strictly using `Arctic Cyan #50A0F0` (No glow, pure data visualization). X/Y axis labels in `Fira Code`, colored `Frost White #E0ECF4` at 0.6 opacity.

#### Responsive Behavior (Mobile-First < 768px)
On mobile, the 12-column grid naturally collapses into a 1-column stack (`display: flex; flex-direction: column; gap: 16px;`).
*   Because we are using distinct `Carbon` and `Graphite` cards against the `Obsidian Black #0A0A0F` main background, it will *look* like a premium feed on mobile, but retain its Bento identity.
*   Touch targets for any interactive cards will be a minimum of `48px` (exceeding our 44px minimum for luxury spacing).
