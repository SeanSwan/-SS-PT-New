### Design Decisions (Answering the 6 Core Questions)

1. **Border-Radius:** **`6px`**. 8px is slightly too soft for a structured feed, and 0px is too brutalist. `6px` (`0.375rem`) provides that crisp, "square block" Facebook feel while taking the harsh edge off.
2. **Dimensions & Spacing:** Max-width of **`680px`** (optimal line-length for readability). On mobile (<768px), the card spans 100% of the viewport width with `0px` border-radius (flush edges) to maximize screen real estate, switching to `6px` radius and `24px` gap spacing at the 768px breakpoint.
3. **Hero Image Area:** 180px is too short. We will use a fluid height: **`clamp(240px, 40vw, 360px)`**. It will feature a `linear-gradient` overlay at the bottom to seamlessly blend the image into the `#111122` card body. For no-image posts, a centered, low-opacity (5%) Swan watermark with `mix-blend-mode: screen` will sit on a cosmic gradient mesh.
4. **Typography & Color:**
   - Author: `16px`, `600` weight, `#FFFFFF`.
   - Meta/Time: `13px`, `400` weight, `#A0A0B0`.
   - Body: `15px`, `400` weight, `1.6` line-height, `#E0E0E0`.
5. **Reaction Buttons:** Flexbox row, `gap: 8px`. Minimum touch target **`44px`**.
   - Thumbs Up: Swan Cyan Muted (`#60C0F0`)
   - Heart: NASM Pulse Pink (`#EC4899`)
   - Swan: Wing Purple (`#8B5CF6`)
6. **Border Glow vs. Shadow:** **Shadow + Inner Stroke**. A heavy outer glow looks cheap. We will use a deep drop shadow (`0 8px 32px rgba(0,0,0,0.4)`) combined with a subtle 1px inner border (`rgba(255,255,255,0.06)`) to define the edge against the `#0a0a1a` background. We will reserve a *very subtle* cyan glow strictly for the hover state.

### 2. Responsive Behavior (10 Breakpoints)

*   **320px, 375px, 430px (Mobile):** Card width `100%`. `border-radius: 0`. Border left/right removed. Flush against the screen edges. Vertical gap between posts: `12px`.
*   **768px (Tablet):** Card width `100%` (max `680px`). `border-radius: 6px`. Borders restored. Vertical gap: `24px`.
*   **1024px, 1280px (Desktop):** Feed centers in a grid layout. Max-width remains `680px` to prevent eye strain.
*   **1440px, 1920px, 2560px, 3840px (Ultra-Wide):** Feed remains constrained to `680px` wide column, but sidebars (profile stats, suggested trainers) appear alongside it.

### 3. Animations & Interactions

*   **Entry Reveal:** Staggered fade-up. `animation: slideUpFade 0.5s cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1) forwards;`
*   **Card Hover:** Transform `translateY(-2px)` with a transition of `300ms ease`. Box-shadow expands slightly, and border color shifts to `rgba(0, 255, 255, 0.15)` (Subtle Swan Cyan glow).
*   **Reaction Click:** Spring physics. Scale down to `0.85`, then bounce to `1.1`, then settle at `1.0`. `cubic-bezier(0.175, 0.885, 0.32, 1.275)`.

### 4. Accessibility

*   **Touch Targets:** All reaction buttons and the author profile link are explicitly set to `min-height: 44px` and `min-width: 44px`.
*   **ARIA:** `aria-label="Like post"`, `aria-label="Love post"`, `aria-label="Swan post"`.
*   **Contrast:** `#A0A0B0` on `#111122` yields a 4.8:1 contrast ratio (WCAG AA compliant for small text).
*   **Reduced Motion:** `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` disables the translateY hover and spring animations, falling back to simple opacity fades.
