### 1. Visual Design Architecture

**Placement & Layout**
- **Client View:** The feedback UI will be a single, pill-shaped glassmorphism container positioned at the **top-right** of the photo card (`top: 12px`, `right: 12px`). This keeps it far away from the "Request Edit/Original" buttons (which sit at the bottom) and aligns with standard mental models for "liking" media.
- **Admin View:** Feedback stats overlay at the bottom-left of admin thumbnails, with a dedicated "Cleanup Suggestions" dashboard panel spanning the top of the grid.

**Color Palette (Crystalline Swan Extension)**
- **Glass Base:** `linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(10, 10, 26, 0.5) 0%, rgba(10, 10, 26, 0.7) 100%)`
- **Glass Border:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`
- **Inactive Icon (Stardust Gray):** `#8A8A9D`
- **Thumbs Up Active (Ice Wing):** `#60C0F0` (with a subtle `drop-shadow(0 0 8px rgba(96, 192, 240, 0.4))`)
- **Thumbs Down Active (Nebula Red):** `#FF5E7E`
- **Admin Cleanup Accent:** `#7851A9` (Cosmic Purple) to `#FF5E7E` gradient border.

**Typography**
- **Vote Counts:** `13px`, Font Weight `600`, Line Height `1`, Letter Spacing `0.5px`. Font family should match our primary UI sans-serif (Inter or system-ui).
- **Admin Panel Header:** `18px`, Font Weight `500`, Color `#FFFFFF`.

**Touch Targets (Crucial)**
- The visual icons will be `20px`, but the interactive `<button>` elements **must be exactly `44px` by `44px`**. 
- The pill container will be `44px` tall to perfectly encapsulate these touch targets, ensuring zero frustration for older users.

---

### 2. Responsive Behavior Matrix

We must support our 10-breakpoint architecture.

- **Mobile (320px, 375px, 430px):** 
  - The feedback pill is **always visible** (opacity: 1). Hover states do not exist here.
  - Pill sits slightly tighter to the edge: `top: 8px`, `right: 8px`.
- **Tablet (768px, 1024px):**
  - Always visible to accommodate hybrid touch/pointer interactions.
- **Desktop/Ultrawide (1280px, 1440px, 1920px, 2560px, 3840px):**
  - The feedback pill defaults to `opacity: 0` and `transform: translateY(-4px)`.
  - On photo card hover, it transitions to `opacity: 1` and `transform: translateY(0)`.
  - If a user has *already voted* on a photo, the pill remains **always visible** at `opacity: 0.8` (fading to `1` on hover) so they can see their active state.

---

### 3. Animations & Interactions

We are avoiding generic linear fades. We use premium spring physics.

- **Easing Curve (Swan Spring):** `cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)`
- **Hover State (Desktop):** The individual thumb button scales to `1.1` with a `0.2s ease-out` transition.
- **Tap/Click Micro-interaction:** 
  - On click, the icon scales down to `0.8`.
  - On release, it pops to `1.25`, fills with the active color (Ice Wing or Nebula Red), and settles back to `1.0` using the Swan Spring curve.
- **Count Increment:** When a vote is cast, the number slides up (`translateY(-10px)` -> `opacity: 0`), and the new number slides in from the bottom (`translateY(10px)` -> `translateY(0)`).

---

### 4. Accessibility (A11y)

Claude, do not skip these. This is a premium platform.
- **ARIA Labels:** `<button aria-label="Thumbs up, 12 votes" aria-pressed={hasVotedUp}>`
- **Focus Management:** `:focus-visible` must trigger a `2px solid #00FFFF` outline with a `2px` offset. Do not use standard browser blue rings.
- **Color Contrast:** The Ice Wing (`#60C0F0`) and Nebula Red (`#FF5E7E`) against the dark glass background (`#0a0a1a` at 70% opacity) exceed the WCAG AA 4.5:1 ratio.
