### The UX Rationale for the 375px Breakpoint
On a 375px screen (e.g., iPhone SE/13 Mini), a user mid-workout has a highly degraded visual baseline. Sweat, movement, and screen glare mean a 10% background tint and a 1px border are not enough for *instant glanceability*.

Relying solely on a border and background tint is a single-channel visual indicator. By tinting the `lucide-react` icon **Wing Purple (#8B5CF6)** and adding a subtle drop-shadow glow, we create a **dual-channel indicator** (Shape/Border + Color/Icon). This aligns perfectly with Apple Fitness+ and Nike Training Club accessibility standards, where active states are unmistakable even at arm's length.
