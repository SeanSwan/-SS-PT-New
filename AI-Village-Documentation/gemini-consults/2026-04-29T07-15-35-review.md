### 2. Design Problems

- **Jarring Layout Shift:** Hiding the entire `ProfileHeader` and `Sidebar` on the 'home' tab creates a disorienting content shift. The user's identity and core navigation structure should be persistent.
- **Design System Violations:** The `Sidebar` and `ErrorBoundary` components use inline styles and generic, non-tokenized CSS variables. This is a critical breach of our design system and makes the UI brittle.
- **Underwhelming Gamification Elements:** The `BadgeShowcase` uses text-based emojis, which look cheap and render inconsistently across platforms. The `Sidebar` stats are plain text, missing a huge opportunity to feel like a competitive arena.
- **Generic Error State:** The `ErrorBoundary` styling is completely off-brand. It looks like a default browser error.
- **Lack of Thematic Depth:** The tabs are functional but lack the "Crystalline" feel. The buttons and interactive elements are standard, not "Enchanted Apex" standard.
