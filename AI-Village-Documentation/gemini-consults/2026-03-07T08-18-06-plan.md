### 1. Design Gaps (What You Missed)

*   **The "Empty State" Experience:** You mentioned the flow is "open workspace → select client", but what is on the screen *before* the drawer opens? A blank screen is unacceptable. We need a premium "Select Client" empty state that acts as the primary call-to-action when `clientId` is null.
*   **Banner Real Estate:** A persistent "Selected Client banner at the top" will consume too much vertical space on mobile (320px/375px). It needs to be a floating glassmorphic pill on desktop, and a compact, sticky glass header on mobile.
*   **Drawer Trigger / Context Switching:** Once a client is selected and the drawer closes, how does the user change clients? The "Selected Client" header must double as the trigger to re-open the drawer.
*   **Visual Hierarchy in the Drawer:** "Search, sessions remaining, last workout" is good data, but needs hierarchy. If a trainer has 50 clients, scanning must be instantaneous. We need avatar placeholders (cosmic gradients), clear typography distinctions, and active/inactive status indicators.
*   **Accessibility & Focus Management:** When the drawer opens, focus must trap inside it. The background must be obscured by a backdrop, and the `Escape` key must close it.

### 2. Enhancement Opportunities (Elevating the UX)

*   **The Apple Fitness+ / Peloton Polish:** Inside the drawer, the "Sessions Remaining" shouldn't just be text. It should be a high-contrast Swan Cyan pill (`#00FFFF` text on `rgba(0, 255, 255, 0.1)` background).

*   **Mobile Bottom Sheet Physics:** On mobile (`< 1024px`), the bottom sheet must support swipe-to-close gestures using Framer Motion's `drag="y"` properties, complete with a 4px high, 40px wide pill indicator at the top of the sheet.
*   **Workspace Transition:** When a client is selected, the `WorkoutLogger` or `PlanBuilder` shouldn't just snap into view. It needs a subtle stagger fade-in (`opacity: 0` to `1`, `y: 10` to `0`).
