### 5. Accessibility (A11y) Requirements

This is non-negotiable for our older demographic:
*   **Contrast:** Ice Wing (`#60C0F0`) on Midnight Sapphire (`#002060`) yields a contrast ratio of ~7.5:1 (Passes AAA). Gilded Fern (`#C6A84B`) yields ~6.2:1 (Passes AA).
*   **ARIA Roles:** 
    *   The Modal must have `role="dialog"` and `aria-modal="true"`.
    *   The Cart Drawer must have `role="complementary"` and `aria-label="Your Selected Photos"`.
*   **Focus Trapping:** When the Modal or Drawer is open, keyboard focus *must* be trapped inside.
*   **Focus States:** All buttons and inputs must have a highly visible focus ring: `&:focus-visible { outline: 3px solid #60C0F0; outline-offset: 2px; }`.
