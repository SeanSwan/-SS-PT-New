### 1. Design Gaps & Vetoes (What You Missed)

*   **VETO: "Red Tint" for 0-Session Scheduling (Feature 5)**
    *   *Why:* Red implies an error, a destructive action, or a failure. Upselling a client to buy more sessions is a *positive* conversion event.
    *   *Correction:* We will use a **"Premium Lock" state**. The schedule block will have a heavy frosted glass overlay (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`), a glowing Swan Cyan lock icon, and a "Secure Your Session" CTA.
*   **GAP: VIP Card Psychology (Feature 1)**
    *   *Why:* A custom package shouldn't just look like another store item. It is a bespoke "SwanStudios Special." It needs to feel like being handed a metal black card.
    *   *Correction:* The VIP card requires a holographic/iridescent hover effect and a distinct dark-purple cosmic gradient, separating it entirely from standard packages.
*   **GAP: Mobile-First Data Tables (Feature 2)**
    *   *Why:* Standard data tables for the Users section will break our 320px/375px breakpoints.
    *   *Correction:* At breakpoints `320px` to `768px`, users must render as **Glassmorphism Profile Cards**. At `1024px` and above, they transition into a sleek, borderless data grid.
*   **GAP: AI Generation State (Feature 3)**
    *   *Why:* AI generation takes time. A generic spinner is "AI slop."
    *   *Correction:* We need a "Cosmic Synthesis" loading state—a pulsing nebula effect while the AI Village creates the workout.

### 2. Enhancement Opportunities (Elevating the UX)

*   **The VIP Calculator Layout:** For the Admin Custom Package creator, use a split-screen layout on desktop (`1024px+`). Left side: Input controls (sliders for bonus sessions). Right side: A real-time, sticky preview of the VIP Card the client will see. As the admin adds free sessions, the effective hourly rate on the card updates with a slot-machine number roll animation.
*   **Messaging Micro-interactions:** Implement a "breathing" typing indicator. Instead of three generic dots, use three tiny stars that pulse with a Swan Cyan glow.

### 3. Architecture Recommendations

*   **Animation Strategy:**
