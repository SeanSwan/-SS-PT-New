### **1. Design Quality Assessment**

**Current Score: 6.5/10**

**Reasoning:**
The foundation is solid. The component structure is logical, the use of `framer-motion` is appropriate, and the responsive breakpoints are well-defined. The layout is functional and doesn't have any major usability flaws.

However, it currently lacks the soul of the **Crystalline Swan** theme. ### **2. Design Problems**

I've identified four key areas where the current design falls short of our premium standard.

1.  **CRITICAL: Pervasive Color Token Violations.** The code is littered with hardcoded hex values and generic CSS variables instead of our official Crystalline Swan tokens. This is a direct violation of our design system, creates inconsistency, and makes future theme updates impossible.
2.  **HIGH: Underdeveloped and Generic Interactivity.** Hover and focus states are basic. The "shine" effect on `StatItem` and `UserRole` is a common trope. Our buttons lack the specified dual-glow effect and feel generic. The design doesn't feel tactile, crystalline, or luxurious.
