### 1. Design Gaps (What You Missed)

*   **Lack of Depth & Refraction:** Crystals are defined by how they interact with light. Your plan mentions background and primary colors but completely ignores surface elevation, borders, and shadows. We need inner shadows (to mimic facets) and outer glows (to mimic luminescence).
*   **Corporate Light Theme:** Using `#2563EB` (standard Tailwind blue) on a white background for "Arctic Dawn" will look like a generic banking app, not a premium fitness platform. We need an icy, vibrant cyan/blue that feels energetic.
*   **State Transitions:** How does a crystalline button react when pressed? It shouldn't just darken. It needs to "refract" (shift its gradient and inner shadow) to provide tactile feedback.
*   **Typography & Contrast:** Crystalline themes risk severe accessibility failures if text is placed over translucent glass without proper text-shadows or contrast ratios. 

### 2. Enhancement Opportunities (Elevating the UX)

*   **The "Swan Glass" Effect:** Instead of solid hex backgrounds for cards and navbars, we will use heavy background blurs with low-opacity fills and sharp, 1px semi-transparent borders. This creates the "ice/crystal" aesthetic.
*   **Micro-interactions:** Buttons and interactive cards should use a custom cubic-bezier easing curve to feel snappy and responsive, mimicking the physical exertion of a workout.
*   **Facetted Gradients:** Instead of linear 2-stop gradients, we will use multi-stop gradients with sharp color stops to create a "faceted" 3D look on primary elements.
*   **Premium Dark Mode:** "Void Crystal" should feel like deep space with sharp, neon-cyan laser accents. It should feel intense and focused, perfect for high-intensity training sessions.

### 3. Architecture Recommendations

*   **Theme Token Strategy:** Do not hardcode hexes in the styled-components. Expand our theme interface to include semantic tokens for refraction and glass:
    *   `theme.surface.glass`
    *   `theme.border.facet`
    *   `theme.shadow.glow`
    *   `theme.shadow.innerRefract`
*   **Styled-Component Mixins:** Create a `mixins.ts` file exporting reusable CSS snippets for the glass effect so we don't repeat `backdrop-filter` 600 times.
*   **Animation Approach:** Use **CSS Transforms & Transitions** for hover/active states (performance is critical for users on mobile devices at the gym). Reserve **Framer Motion** strictly for page mounting, modal pop-ups, and layout shifts.
