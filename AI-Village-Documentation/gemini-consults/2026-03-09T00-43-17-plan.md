*   **Touch Target A11y:** You mentioned replacing hearts with swans, but didn't specify the hit area. The SVG itself will be ~24x24px, but the clickable wrapper *must* be `44x44px` minimum to meet our mobile-first a11y standards.

*   **Haptic & Visual Feedback:** A social feed without micro-interactions feels dead. The "Like" action needs a specific animation curve to feel satisfying.

### 2. Enhancement Opportunities
*   **Terminology:** 
    *   Followers -> **"The Flock"** (Noun)

### 3. Architecture Recommendations
*   **Styled-Components:** Use transient props (e.g., `$isElevated`, `$isFlying`) to prevent boolean attributes from leaking into the DOM.
*   **Animation Engine:** Use **Framer Motion** for the Swan "Elevate" interaction and feed entrance animations. Use standard CSS `transition: all 0.3s cubic-bezier(0.25, 0.8, 0.25, 1)` for simple hover states.
*   **Glassmorphism Tokens:** Standardize our glass panels.
