## Design Review: Home V-Next Hero Optics

### 1. Design Quality Assessment (Current State)

**Rating: 7/10**

**Reasoning:**
The foundational work is excellent. The animation concept is sound, the SVG facets are crisp, and the performance considerations are exactly what I expect from SwanStudios. The component structure is logical and maintainable.

However, the execution of the final visual layer—specifically the CTAs, typography, and atmospheric depth—feels generic. The buttons lack brand identity and visual weight, undermining their purpose. The overall scene feels slightly flat, missing the crystalline depth and ethereal glow that should define this hero. It follows the instructions but doesn't yet capture the *soul* of the Crystalline Swan theme.

### 2. Design Problems

I've identified four key areas for improvement, ranging from critical branding failures to opportunities for premium polish.

-   **CRITICAL:** The Call-to-Action (CTA) buttons are visually weak and off-brand. They don't use our primary color system and lack the "premium" feel required.
-   **HIGH:** The typographic hierarchy is incorrect. The sub-headline uses a display font weight and family, creating visual competition with the main headline.
-   **MEDIUM:** The scene lacks atmospheric depth. The background scrim and facet glow are too simple, resulting in a flat composition.
-   **LOW:** Micro-interactions on the CTAs are basic (`brightness`). We can do much better to create a satisfying, tactile experience.

### 3. Design Solutions & 4. Elevation Opportunities

Here is my vision for correcting these issues and elevating the design.
