We are dealing with parents looking at photos of their children. They are in a high-emotion, high-dopamine state. If we force them through a clinical, multi-page PT onboarding wizard *before* they get what they actually want (the unlocked photos), we will kill the conversion rate.

### 1. Design Gaps & Answers to Your Questions

*   **Q1 & Q2: Signup vs. Stripe & UX Flow:**
    *   **Directive:** Signup MUST happen *before* Stripe, but it must be a **frictionless, in-context Glassmorphic Modal**. Do NOT redirect them to `/onboarding` or `/signup`. Keep them over the gallery. We pre-fill the email, ask for a Password and Phone Number (for SMS session reminders), and immediately transition the modal state to the Stripe Elements checkout.
*   **Q3: Post-Purchase Landing:**
    *   **Directive:** Do NOT redirect them to the PT Dashboard immediately. That breaks the emotional loop. Post-purchase, the modal transitions to a "Cosmic Success" state.
    *   **Primary CTA:** "View Unlocked Gallery" (closes modal, reveals the now-VIP gallery with a subtle cyan flash animation).
    *   **Secondary CTA (Glowing):** "Book Your VIP PT Session" (opens the Dashboard/Schedule in a *new tab*).
*   **Q4: Presenting the PT Service:**
    *   **Directive:** Use a horizontal, swipeable "Trust Card" carousel inside the checkout modal (mobile) or a sleek 2-column grid (desktop). Highlight the **NASM-Certified** badge prominently. Parents need to know Sean is a 25-year veteran, not a weekend warrior.
*   **Q6: Existing Users:**
    *   **Directive:** On email input blur, if the email exists, smoothly animate the password field into a "Log In to Claim VIP" state. No page reloads.

### 2. Enhancement Opportunities

*   **The "Galaxy-Swan" VIP Reveal:** When payment succeeds, we don't just show a checkmark. We trigger a Framer Motion sequence: the modal's glass border glows Swan Cyan (`#00FFFF`), a subtle SVG swan motif draws itself via `stroke-dasharray`, and a cosmic particle effect emits from the center.
*   **Trust Signals:** The Stripe input area must be encased in a subtle Cosmic Purple (`#7851A9`) glow to indicate security. We will use a custom NASM-certified SVG badge next to the price.
*   **Micro-interactions:** All buttons must have a magnetic hover effect (desktop) and a tactile scale-down tap effect (mobile).

### 3. Architecture Recommendations

*   **Component Structure:**
    *   `VIPConversionModal.tsx` (Orchestrator)
    *   `VIPAccountStep.tsx` (Pre-filled email, password, existing user detection)
    *   `VIPCheckoutStep.tsx` (Stripe Elements wrapped in our theme)
    *   `VIPSuccessStep.tsx` (The dopamine hit + routing)
*   **Animation Strategy:** Use `framer-motion` for all step transitions.

*   **Styled-Components:** Strictly adhere to our token system. No hardcoded colors outside of the theme provider.
