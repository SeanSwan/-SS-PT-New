### 2. Collapsible Width & Breakpoint Logic (Correction to 48px)
**Reject 48px collapsed width.** 
If our minimum touch target is 44px, a 48px sidebar leaves only 2px of padding on either side. It will look cramped, unrefined, and "choked." 

*   **Expanded Width:** `240px` (≥ 1280px breakpoint)
*   **Collapsed Width:** `72px` (1024px – 1279px breakpoint). This allows a 48x48px icon container centered within the 72px width, giving us 12px of luxurious breathing room on the left and right.
*   **Mobile/Tablet (< 1024px):** The vertical sidebar transforms into a horizontal scrollable sub-nav (pill-shaped tabs) just below the main header to preserve vertical screen real estate.

### 3. Visual Design & Interaction Specs
We are using **Carbon (#141419)** for the sidebar background to match the card darks, sitting on top of the **Obsidian Black (#0A0A0F)** main background.

*   **Active State:** Background shifts to a translucent **Midnight Sapphire (#002060)** at 40% opacity. Text/Icon turns **Frost White (#E0ECF4)**. A 3px left border of **Wing Purple (#8B5CF6)** appears, casting a subtle **Ice Wing (#60C0F0)** box-shadow glow into the button.
*   **Hover State:** Background shifts to `rgba(224, 236, 244, 0.05)` (Frost White 5%).

*   **Transition:** `all 0.3s cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)` (premium ease-out).
