### 2. Responsive Behavior (10 Breakpoints)

*   **320px - 430px (Mobile)**:
    *   Grid collapses to `grid-template-columns: 1fr;`.
    *   `max-height: 500px` is **removed** (`max-height: none`).
    *   Tabs become a horizontal scrolling row (`overflow-x: auto; scroll-snap-type: x mandatory;`).
    *   Touch targets for tabs strictly `min-height: 44px`.
*   **768px (Tablet Portrait)**:
    *   Grid remains `1fr`, but internal lists cap at 5 items to save vertical space.
*   **1024px (Tablet Landscape) - 1440px (Desktop)**:
    *   Standard `300px 1fr` split. `max-height: 500px` enforced.
*   **1920px - 3840px (Ultra-Wide)**:
    *   Grid expands to `350px 1fr`.
    *   Live Counter font size scales to `64px`.
    *   `max-height` scales to `600px` (at 2560px) and `800px` (at 3840px).

### 3. Animations & Interactions

*   **Live Pulse**: The green/cyan dot next to "Active Now" must use a dual-ring pulse. Inner dot static, outer ring scales 1 to 2.5 and fades opacity 0.8 to 0 over 2 seconds (`infinite ease-out`).
*   **Bar Chart Fill**: When the component mounts, the background bars for Countries/Cities must scale from `width: 0%` to their target width using `cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)` over 800ms.
*   **Tab Transitions**: Switching tabs (Countries -> Cities -> Recent) triggers a `fadeSlideUp` (opacity 0->1, translateY 10px->0) over 300ms.
*   **Row Hover**: Hovering over a top page or timeline row translates the text `translateX(4px)` over 200ms `ease` and applies a `rgba(0, 255, 255, 0.08)` background.

*   **Tabs**: Must use `role="tablist"`, `role="tab"`, `aria-selected={true/false}`, and `aria-controls`.
*   **Reduced Motion**: Wrap all keyframes in `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)`. If preferred, disable the pulse and bar fill animations.
