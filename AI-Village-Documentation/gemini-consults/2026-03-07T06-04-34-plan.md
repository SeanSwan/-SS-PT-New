### 1. Design Gaps (What You Missed)

*   **The Monolith Problem:** You cannot build a premium, fluid 3-click booking flow inside a 947-line component. We must decouple the calendar grid, the booking interface, and the state management.
*   **Timezone Elegance:** Trainers and clients are often in different timezones. The UI must elegantly display the trainer's timezone with a subtle, auto-calculated conversion for the client.
*   **Email/SMS Design Constraints:** You mentioned templates, but styled-components don't work in emails. We need strict, inline-CSS HTML templates for emails that maintain the Galaxy-Swan dark theme across email clients (which notoriously strip out complex CSS). SMS needs strict character limits to prevent multi-message splitting.
*   **Empty States:** What does the client see when they have no upcoming sessions? A blank screen is unacceptable. We need a "Cosmic Void" empty state that gently guides them to request a session.
*   **Accessibility (a11y) on Glass:** Glassmorphism often fails WCAG contrast ratios. Text on glass panels must have a minimum `rgba(0,0,0,0.4)` underlay to ensure the white/cyan text pops.

### 2. Enhancement Opportunities (Elevating the UX)

*   **The "Swan Glide" Quick-Book Flow:** Instead of a jarring modal popup, we will use a contextual Side Drawer on desktop (1024px+) and a Bottom Sheet on mobile (320px–768px).
    *   *Click 1:* Trainer taps an empty time slot on the calendar. (Drawer slides in).
    *   *Click 2:* Trainer selects the Client from an auto-focused, searchable dropdown.
    *   *Click 3:* Trainer taps "Confirm & Ignite" (Books session, triggers Twilio/SendGrid).
*   **Micro-interactions:** When a session is booked, the time slot should pulse once with a Swan Cyan (`#00FFFF`) glow before settling into its reserved state.
*   **Client-Facing View (The "Orbit"):** Clients shouldn't see a complex calendar. They should see a sleek, vertically scrolling timeline of "Upcoming Orbits" (sessions). Think Apple Wallet boarding passes—large typography, clear dates, and a massive, glowing "Join Virtual Studio" button that activates 15 minutes before the session.

We are ripping apart `UniversalMasterSchedule`. Here is the new styled-component architecture:

**Animation Strategy:**
Use **Framer Motion** for layout shifts (drawer sliding, cards mounting). Use **CSS Transitions** for micro-interactions (hover states, focus rings).
*   *Standard Easing:* `cubic-bezier(0.25, 1, 0.5, 1)` (Smooth, premium deceleration).
