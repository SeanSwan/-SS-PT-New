### 3. ANIMATIONS & INTERACTIONS

* **Tier Badge Float:** Continuous `translateY` floating animation (duration: 6s, easing: `ease-in-out`).
* **Progress Bar Fill:** On mount, bar fills from 0 to current XP.
  * Easing: `cubic-bezier(0.175, 0.885, 0.32, 1.275)` (slight overshoot/bounce).
  * Duration: `1.5s`.
* **Card Hover:**
  * `transform: translateY(-4px) scale(1.02);`
  * Box-shadow transitions to the rarity color glow (e.g., `0 8px 24px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.3)` for Epic).
* **Legendary Shimmer:** Legendary cards have a pseudo-element with a 45-degree white gradient that translates across the card every 3 seconds.

### 4. ACCESSIBILITY

* **Progress Bar:** Must use `role="progressbar"`, `aria-valuenow={currentXP}`, `aria-valuemin="0"`, `aria-valuemax={tierMaxXP}`.
* **Focus States:** `:focus-visible` must have `outline: 2px solid #00FFFF; outline-offset: 4px; border-radius: 8px;`.
* **Reduced Motion:** Wrap all animations in `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` to disable floats and shimmers, and set progress bar transition to `0s`.
