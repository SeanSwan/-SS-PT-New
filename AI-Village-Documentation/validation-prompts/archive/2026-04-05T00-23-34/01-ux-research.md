## UX Research Insights for SwanStudios Feature Upgrade Plan

**Insight:** SwanStudios' core features align with industry standards, but its unique differentiators (NASM OPT 5-phase periodization, voice-first AI coach, Octalysis gamification) require superior execution in UI/UX to truly stand out. Competitors offer robust workout builders, comprehensive client management, and varying degrees of gamification and AI integration.

**Priority:** HIGH
