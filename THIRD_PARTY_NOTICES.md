# THIRD PARTY NOTICES

> **What this is (R2 ruling R-4, 2026-08-18, extends the Fable D4 ruling of 2026-07-25):**
> lifted third-party component code is **dependency hygiene, not a design-ledger matter.**
> Every UI component adapted from an external source (21st.dev, Aceternity, ReactBits, or
> any snippet) gets ONE row here — license verified **per component** (aggregators carry
> heterogeneous licenses; "the site is mostly MIT" is not verification), copyright carried,
> version/commit pinned. **MIT's attribution requirement survives adaptation into Swan
> tokens** — restyling a component does not erase its notice.
>
> Rules of use:
> - **No skill loads this file as design doctrine.** It is a legal record, not taste input.
> - Reference **URLs** stay out of committed docs (`node scripts/redact-urls.mjs`); the
>   provenance identifier below (`source/component · license · verified date`) is what
>   indemnity actually needs.
> - A component whose license cannot be verified **does not ship.** Pro/paid-tier
>   components from mixed-tier aggregators require their own license check — free-tier
>   verification does not transfer.
> - The design ledger may record a *judgment* about a component ("this interaction
>   pattern tested well"), never a transcription of its structure.
