## Suggestions & Enhancements (Phase 2 — grill-me's recommendations)
1. **CTA landing surface must exist BEFORE video #1.** Every training video points at "free assessment at sswanstudios.com" — verify a prospect (not-logged-in) can actually reach the Movement Analysis wizard from a public URL, or that CTA is a dead promise. Pre-launch check, load-bearing.
2. **Wire assessment submissions into speed-to-lead.** The locked Marketing Brain first epic is speed-to-lead email; assessment leads from YouTube must trigger it (fast follow-up is the whole game on a warm lead). Otherwise the channel fills a bucket nobody empties.
3. **Proof-pillar consent workflow before the first proof video.** A one-page consent template + a "consent on file?" gate in the pre-flight checklist. No consent = no video is already doctrine; the workflow makes it operational.
4. **Pillar playlists from day one.** Four playlists matching the four pillars — watch-through coherence is how YouTube learns who to recommend the channel to, and it costs nothing.
5. **(Later, C4 cadence) Weekly content-plan automation.** Once 3-4 videos exist, a Hermes cron drafts the next week's "one shoot → four assets" plan into the outbox for Sean's morning review. Not yet — after the first manual batches prove the shape.
