Your assistant

On your laptop · Private · Offline

To open it: double-click Assistant on your Desktop. That's it — no typing, no setup.

Just paste your day in

  • Any messFive things in one paragraph is exactly right. It sorts them.

Or ask for

  • Sort thisthe main one
  • What's most important here?puts it in order
  • Daily noteswording for families, from what you told it
  • Help me plan tomorrowrealistic, not Pinterest
  • I'm overwhelmed, here's everything…must / should / extra
  • What did I get done?from this conversation
It has no memory. Every time you open it, it starts fresh — it genuinely does not know about yesterday, and it will tell you so rather than make something up. Start a new conversation each evening.

Names go here, not on your phone. This can't send anything anywhere. Your phone's notes app can.

Something happened? Write it on the paper incident form, today, before you leave. The chat is never the record.

Big dumps take about a minute. Keep it plugged in.

If it mixes up two children — just correct it, or start a fresh conversation. Not your fault, not broken.

When you finish, press Cmd+K then Cmd+W. Every night. It clears the window so today's notes aren't sitting on screen tomorrow.
