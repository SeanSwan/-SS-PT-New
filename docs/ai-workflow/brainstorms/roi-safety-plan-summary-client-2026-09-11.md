## What you're getting, in one sentence

A system that checks three government registries every week, pulls every business contact they
publish, merges everything into one clean spreadsheet, and tells you exactly what's new — so you
stop doing it by hand every month.

## How it works — 4 simple steps

1. **COLLECT** — Every week, the system automatically reads the same three sources you check by
   hand today: California SMARTS (storm-water permits), EPA TRI (Toxics Release Inventory), and
   EPA RCRAInfo (hazardous-waste handlers). All public data. No logins, no workarounds.
2. **CLEAN & MERGE** — It combines the three lists, removes duplicates (the same facility often
   appears in two or three registries), and keeps a link back to the official source record for
   every contact.
3. **COMPARE** — It compares this week against last week and flags every **new** or **changed**
   contact automatically.
4. **DELIVER** — You get an updated spreadsheet you own, plus a short weekly email: what was
   checked, what changed, and anything worth a look.

## What you receive every week

- The current full contact list (company, contact name, role, email, phone, address, source ID)
- A "New & Updated" list — this week's changes, ready to work
- A weekly summary email with each source's status
- Full history, so any change can be explained later
- If a government site is down, it tells you — and keeps serving last week's good data
