## CANDIDATE v1 (all 17 amendments merged by Fable)

**1 — Shoulder click+ache, wants harder push day.** Clicking alone is common and benign; clicking WITH ache under load means we stop loading that range today. First screen: any trauma or prior dislocation → that's an assessment before we program at all [A-GLM1]. Otherwise she still gets a hard push day — landmine press, neutral-grip at/below shoulder height, horizontal pressing. If the substitution also provokes symptoms, stop — we don't test variations hunting for one that works [A-QWEN1]. Ask: duration, ache at rest vs load, trend. Non-urgent physio/GP referral — not same-day — for night pain, loss of range, or weakness that doesn't warm up [A-GLM1]. Log it.

**2 — Knee swollen 4 days, buckled on stairs.** Stop — buckling is a stability failure, not soreness, and leg extensions load the joint directly; they don't dodge it. Ask mechanism: pop, twist, locking [A-GLM2]. No lower-body work that side today; upper/unaffected fine. Tier: physio or sports-med review before her next lower session — not the ER, absent these: knee locked, can't bear weight → same-day; calf pain with the swelling → same-day (clot caution, stated without diagnosing) [A-GLM2]. Documented.

**3 — Low back seized post-deadlift, can't bend.** Cancel the 5pm. Not bed rest — gentle movement and normal daily activity as tolerated; staying still tends to make it worse [A-GLM3/A-KIMI1, verify vs current LBP guideline]. Emergency care NOW for: groin/saddle numbness, bladder or bowel changes, progressive leg weakness. Routine check-in in a few days; formal review if not clearly easing inside 1–2 weeks [A-KIMI1]. I asked the red-flag questions and his answers were negative — that goes in the log verbatim; the message itself is documentation [A-GLM3]. Also screening: fever, history of cancer, unexplained weight loss, or significant trauma changes the tier [A-GLM3, verify].

**4 — Dizzy + visual spots mid-circuit, finished anyway.** Finishing was the wrong call — dizziness with visual disturbance is a terminate-now, don't-resume symptom. Then the screen the draft skipped: was there an identifiable benign cause — heat, skipped meals, poor sleep, breath-holding under load? Single episode + clear benign cause + full resolution → fix the cause, resume next session, no physician gate [A-GLM4]. Unexplained, recurrent, or with chest discomfort, palpitations, or fainting → clinician before the next hard session; same-day if it recurs. Documented with date either way.

**5 — Electric-shock pain down leg on hinge.** That's neural, not a tight hamstring — and stretching it is contraindicated because you'd be adding tension to an already-irritated nerve, likely worsening it; that's why the training-buddy fix is wrong [A-KIMI2]. No hinging, no self-prescribed nerve glides either [A-GLM5]. She keeps moving — walking and non-provocative training, not rest [A-GLM5]. Assessment at days-speed; immediate care for foot numbness/weakness, worsening, or any saddle/bladder/bowel change.

**6 — 4wk post rotator-cuff repair, "ease back in."** "Ease back in" is not a program and I don't reverse-engineer surgical rehab. I need the written post-op protocol or direct contact with his rehab clinician before that shoulder sees load. Meanwhile: NOT "everything else" — no running, jumping, heavy carries, or loaded grip on that side; all of them load or traction a healing repair [A-GLM6]. Lower body and far-side work that doesn't provoke it. If clearance stays verbal, I document his account of it verbatim and program to the most conservative reading [A-GLM6].

**7 — Wrist grinds on push-ups, thumb numb after.** The numbness is the signal — nerve, not joint noise — and painless doesn't mean benign. This is a MODIFICATION, not an exercise removal [A-GLM7/A-KIMI3]: neutral-wrist pressing (handles, dumbbells) continues today; flat-palm loaded wrist extension is what comes out. If symptoms occur ONLY during the movement and resolve with the neutral-wrist change, we monitor. Referral — routine, not urgent — if numbness recurs despite modification, persists after sessions, shows at night, weakens grip, or spreads [A-QWEN2].

**8 — Chest tightness + winded on stairs, ready for HIIT.** Step zero, before anything: is he symptomatic RIGHT NOW at rest? Yes → emergency services, EAP in motion, AED location known [A-GLM8]. No → he still does not train today, and this is the one I am never talked out of: exertional chest tightness with breathlessness gets medical review before we train again. Radiating to jaw/arm/back, sweating, nausea → emergency, not appointment. If he refuses and leaves, that refusal is documented [A-GLM8]. Follow up personally.

**9 — Fever, 30-day streak.** Session's off. Fever means systemic illness — training worsens recovery, and the gym gets his germs. [Business-judgment label, not safety protocol [A-GLM9/A-KIMI4]:] if the streak matters, a logged recovery day can count toward it — my call as coach, not a safety rule. Return: ≥24h fever-free, eating normally, first session deliberately lighter [A-GLM9]. If she arrives febrile, the session is still off [A-GLM9]. Fever itself escalates to a clinician when high, persistent past a few days, or with chest pain / breathing trouble [A-KIMI4].

**10 — 2nd trimester, heavy KB swings + long supine core.** Two separate calls. Supine core comes out now — extended flat-on-back work in later pregnancy risks supine hypotension: the uterus can compress the vena cava and drop her blood pressure [A-QWEN3]. That's guideline-grounded and doesn't wait for clearance. The heavy ballistic swings: continuing at that load needs actual clearance from her doctor/midwife — "probably fine" isn't clearance. This is NOT "pregnant means see a doctor before exercising" [A-GLM10]: uncomplicated pregnancy trains on, hard, with modification — upright carries, supported strength, modified core, intensity anchored by talk test / moderate RPE [A-GLM10]. Stop-signs any session: bleeding, contractions, fluid leakage, dizziness, chest pain [A-KIMI5/A-GLM10, verify ACOG wording]. Documented, clearance requested in writing.
