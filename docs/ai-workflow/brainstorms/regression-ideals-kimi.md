But an answer teaching the RDL pattern without a spinal-position cue is a trainer flinch; "slides down the thighs" implies proximity, not back position, and a novice can obey every word here with a rounded spine.

> Replace: "The Romanian starts standing and never touches down: soft knees, hips travel BACK, bar slides down the thighs to around mid-shin, then hips drive through."
> With: "The Romanian starts standing and never touches down: soft knees, hips travel BACK, back stays flat, bar slides down the thighs to around mid-shin, then hips drive through."

Three words. Fixes the only real safety-relevant omission in the set without bloating.
