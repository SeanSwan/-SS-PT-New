**F1 (new MAJOR — the defect class the other seats missed: the SAME `.find` bug lives in `normalizeModel`, not just §4).**
Consensus fix (2)/(3) replaces `pkCol=Object.keys(attrs).find(c=>attrs[c].pk)` in the seed loop and rewrites the readers to use `a.foreignKeyTarget.pk`. But `foreignKeyTarget.pk` is *produced* by `normalizeModel()` at the line:
`const pk = Object.keys(tAttrs).find((k) => tAttrs[k].primaryKey) || found.targetKey || 'id';`
That is the identical first-match-only defect: for a composite-PK parent, `foreignKeyTarget.pk` resolves to the first PK column, so even after fix (3) is applied verbatim, `out[col]=parentRow[a.foreignKeyTarget.pk]` and the backfill read `insertedIds[targetLower][0][attrs[bf.col].foreignKeyTarget.pk]` read the WRONG column. Fix (3) as written ships a half-fixed pipeline. `normalizeModel` must also switch to `pkCols=Object.keys(tAttrs).filter(k=>tAttrs[k].primaryKey)` and select the single key matching the child's FK cardinality (or refuse/skip composite targets explicitly). Without this, the consensus's own residual-risk line ("composite-PK paths unverified") is guaranteed-false rather than merely unverified.
