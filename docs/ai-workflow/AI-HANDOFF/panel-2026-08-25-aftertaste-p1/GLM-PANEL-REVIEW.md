Scenario: first real Blender export — indexed, ~40k triangles, 5 accessors → ledger records budget "5," stamped `{tool, command, date, commit}`. The one-triangle fixture coincides (one non-indexed triangle = exactly 1 accessor), which is precisely why B1's "too easy" fear was justified — the fixture hides a semantic bug in the slice's central claim.
