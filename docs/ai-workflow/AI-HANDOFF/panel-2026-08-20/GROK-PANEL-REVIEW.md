## BLOCKERS
1. **P0 — 24h non-repeat is arithmetically unreachable as a covering of this genome space.** Failure: viewer watches continuously, world hold time N seconds → required distinct looks = 86400/N (N=30s → 2880; N=120s → 720; N=600s → 144). Packet §3 cluster counts are n=24 samples, not space cardinality. Occupancy inversion of 18 occupied bins in 24 draws at θ=0.08 gives K≈39 distinguishable looks *per kernel* (`18 ≈ K(1-(1-1/K)^24)`). Three kernels are one grammar (structure ratios 0.92–0.99, §3), so they do not multiply K. Four closed statically-imported kernels, three of them the same machine, ceiling is O(10¹)–O(10²) looks. That is hours of material, not 24h, and grammar recurrence hits before the covering does. Evidence: packet §2 kernel table; §3 cluster table and cross-kernel ratios. No file:line in packet.

2. **P0 — Kernel acceptance gate cannot accept a new grammar.** Failure: add a structurally new kernel (pelagos, structure-only 1.50–1.87 vs feedback family) → combined ratio 1.06–1.11 → reject against the “≥50% vs every existing kernel” bar. Colour is 36 dims at weight 1.0 from a *shared* palette generator, kernel-invariant by construction; motion 12 dims at 0.6; occupancy excluded from distance. The gate is definitionally half-blind to the property it adjudicates and will keep shipping three copies of one feedback machine. Evidence: packet §2 “One generator, shared by every kernel”; §3 findings 1–2 and the pelagos 1.07 fail.

3. **P0 — Descriptor 76 ≠ stated 64, and the missing block is the owner’s taste law.** Failure: distance used for breeding, set-list farthest-first, and kernel gate → worlds ordered/selected as shuffled palettes of the same blob; empty-frame genomes survive; “density to the corners” is unenforced. 36 colour + 16 orientation + 12 motion = 64, not 76. Evidence: packet §3 descriptor breakdown vs “76-dim”; §1 density-to-corners law.

## ATTACKS
- Correctness: Happy-path variety story treats u16^20 as an infinite well; measured manifold is ~40 looks. Cluster table is a sample statistic; using it as a north-star “we have 18” *or* as “space is huge” are both wrong. Canvas-snapshot descriptor on feedback kernels throws away the only temporal state those kernels have (they are advected fields *with history*); pelagos is stateless with a horizon — stills compare attractors to heightfields. GPU nondeterminism: harness run on RTX 5090; owner rejects choppy but product must not depend on a 5090 — iteration/feedback-gain axes are an unstated hang/frame-time path. Reduced-motion is a constraint, not an implemented path in §2. Audio mic/file: no failure path for permission deny, device switch, or silent input (genome still renders; “audio-reactive” becomes a screensaver). 16-term command layer is not granular control; it is a 16-point fuzzy map onto hidden axes, unversioned, no identity. Static `import` of four kernels makes variety a compile-time constant. Farthest-first set-list on a colour-dominated distance is palette shuffle, not a set-list. Identity = vector hashed to world id: hash collision and canonicalisation (endian, axis order) unstated. Tests (504, tsc clean) do not cover the acceptance metric being blind; green CI here is camouflage.

- Security: Standalone WebGL2, no runtime network, no LLM, genomes are bounded numerics — good, and the “never generate shader text” rule must stay. Residual: unbounded or poorly-bounded uniforms (feedback steps, texel fetch count, Gerstner wave count) are a GPU DoS the 5090 will hide and a laptop will surface as the choppiness he rejects. Shareable world ids in a URL are replay/idempotency-ok if they are pure hashes of the vector; if they ever become fetchable resources, that is SSRF/import of untrusted bytes into the GPU path — forbidden by §5 and must not be “just a preset download.” Mic is local-only; do not pipe buffers to any model. Embedding this in the PT SaaS later is a multi-tenant non-issue only while ids are genomes; the moment a world is stored server-side under a user, IDOR on saved worlds is the obvious hole — not in this packet, not designed.

- Data-truth / schema drift: (1) 76-dim vs 36+16+12=64, occupancy excluded — metric schema ≠ advertised schema. (2) Shared OKLab generator vs Crystalline Swan `var(--token,#fallback)` — two palettes, house style will fight Spotlight-variety. (3) Axis groups motion/form/colour/energy vs descriptor colour/orientation/motion — “form” and “energy” have no first-class distance; locks do not lock what the metric sees. (4) Kernel axis arity 20/17/16/17 with per-axis crossover — no inter-kernel axis ontology. (5) Theme does not exist as a type; Favourites persist a genome, so a “look he loves” cannot be named, versioned, or partially locked as a theme. (6) World id = hash(vector), identity ≠ seed — share/restore must round-trip the vector; seed replay would lie. (7) Frontend shape: UI copy (“six scene buttons”, “MilkDrop preset controls”) implies a preset object that §5 forbids importing as equation text — response-shape drift toward a MilkDrop clone.

**Q1 (direct):** No. Not from this architecture, and not by sampling more genomes of the same three feedback machines.
Arithmetic above: ~39 looks/kernel at θ=0.08; ×1 grammar ≠ 24h. Perceived repeat is *grammar recurrence*, not world-id collision. A viewer tires of “energy field advected through itself” in minutes, then pelagos reads as the only other show. Architecture that *could* reach it: (a) dozens of hand-authored, structurally distinct kernels (different machines, not different uniforms) — MilkDrop’s actual trick; (b) time-domain composition inside a world (slow genome trajectories, audio-driven non-palette dynamics, morphs) so 24h is a film, not a covering; (c) stop treating Spotlight as a generative target — those images are authored photographs. Do: kill “more genomes” as the 24h plan this week.

**Q2 (direct):** Not salvageable as governor; keep it as a split diagnostic, drop colour from kernel acceptance, put occupancy in at full weight, do not ship a learned embedding in runtime.
Acceptance must be structure+occupancy+motion, colour excluded (shared generator makes it a constant). Breeding must not maximise this vector. Offline perceptual embedding is allowed by §5 and would be a better *gauge* than 76 hand-rolled dims, but it must not become a fitness function (see Q4). **Q3 (direct):** The bottleneck is an invisible genome — no per-axis surface, no theme type — not GLSL-less grammar authoring and not more English verbs.
He asked for granular control to *create themes and worlds*. That is knobs with names, live on his 5090, one click, lock per axis (not four bags), defaults on what he already loves. **Q4 (direct):** Do not put taste in the fitness function. Promote taste to hard invariants; keep a human in the loop.

**Q6 (direct):** The buried false assumption is that variety is a static covering of a genome space measured by canvas stills — and, deeper, that a generative breeder is his art process.
He wants to *watch* 24h and *claim* the art. MilkDrop beats him because 100 files are 100 programs, blended in time, on audio; Spotlight beats him because it is authored reality.
