# Detailed Interactive Design

Sean expects the craft, depth and interactivity of Human Atlas across his sites.
Use this as a design standard with Mega Blueprints and the current project's design system.
It does not authorize building, deploying, spending, messaging others or installing tooling.

Read `references/teaching-prompt.md` for the full portable operating prompt, process,
scroll/motion patterns, Three.js boundaries, examples and rejection criteria.

Start with a domain-specific centerpiece and useful interactions. Inspect the actual
reference. Preserve its detail, depth, identity and capability when Sean requests reuse.
Organize controls around the object. Simpler controls do not mean a simpler object.
Avoid generic low-detail models, flat card-grid substitutions and decorative 3D without a job.

Use real licensed assets and source. First prove baseline fidelity, then adapt the interface.
Show the actual centerpiece in review artifacts; a schematic is insufficient when that asset's
quality is the user's explicit requirement. Distinguish live reference, wireframe and built integration.

Plan reversible scrolling/camera/selection/reveal behavior, keyboard/touch parity,
reduced motion, data truth, errors, privacy, GPU lifetime and actual device budgets.
Never silently degrade the central asset to meet an arbitrary payload target.

Compare the result against reference scenes and full user tasks before claiming the quality bar.
Follow project-specific stack/branding; do not copy Swan's medical workflows into other sites.
When asked for blueprints, deliver the complete packet and stop before application code.

## Inclusive references and practical reuse — v1.2

Support users of every background through explicit appearance/reference choices, without inferring phenotype from nationality. Separate use-my-data from choose-a-reference modes when both serve the task, and share their authoritative preference across connected surfaces. Preserve the detailed core interaction. When the user allows retaining assets if custom work is impractical, test a bounded derivative sample, record the decision, and continue with the approved source assets. Do not turn a custom-asset aspiration into a mandatory full rebuild or pretend unavailable options exist.

For dense interactive tools, make the common task the default. Preserve centerpiece detail while hiding irrelevant controls and selection targets until requested. Expert classification must not be required to finish the common task. Preserve shared draft and scene when returning.
