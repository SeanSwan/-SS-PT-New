## VERDICT
REVISE — N2-first and “map ≠ oracle” are the right calls, but the plan still specifies a parallel unversioned claim graph that cannot store the verdict it draws, cannot feed the Karpathy/creator lane it promises, and walks W1/W6 straight into the zero-PII-to-LLMs house rule.

## BLOCKERS
1. **P0 — Human verdict has no write path.** Failure: owner clicks “supported / disputed / unresolved” on a cluster (§7.2) → save has nowhere durable to go, or it is jammed into `comment_claim_fact_checks` (comment-lane table, currently 0 rows) and never joins to `claim_cluster`. Evidence: §3 cites `comment_claim_fact_checks` as the verdict column; §5 `claim_cluster` has only `cluster_id`, `canonical_assertion`, `distinct_origins`, `first_seen` — no verdict, notes, actor, or timestamp.

2. **P0 — W1 extractor is unspecified and collides with “zero PII to LLMs (IDs only).”** Failure: news snippets containing person names (Karpathy, officials, victims) are sent to a model stamped in `claim.extractor` → house-rule violation; or no model is chosen → W1 cannot produce rows and W3 maps garbage. Evidence: §5 `extractor "model+version"`; §10.1 “Unaddressed: what extracts”; §9 W6 “Hermes · Codex · Claude”; house rule zero PII to LLMs.

3. **P0 — Ingest has no idempotency key, so N2 poisons every downstream count.** Failure: owner enables 1 RSS connector, poll 2..N re-fetches the same headline/URL → duplicate `news_items` → duplicate `claim` rows → `distinct_origins` inflates and syndication collapse (the load-bearing §3 property) inverts. Evidence: §8 “append-only; nothing rewritten”; §5 no unique `(url, source)` / content hash; §0 already shows empty tables from unfed machinery — duplicates are the next failure mode once a feeder exists.

4. **P1 — `source_item_id` is not a real FK across the durable record.** Failure: a YouTube `creator_item` (Karpathy) or W5 inbound report is extracted → `claim.source_item_id` cannot point at it (ER is `news_items ||--o{ claim`), citations 404, or a polymorphic id silently joins the wrong table. Evidence: §2 ITEM = `news_items / creator_item`; §5 ER only `news_items ||--o{ claim`; §1 “Karpathy Wiki” mapped to W1–W3; §9 W1 depends on N2 only; `creator_item` stays 0 with no enable-slice.

5. **P1 — Schema cannot represent corrections, retractions, or a claim that changes.** Failure: outlet revises “12 dead on the 19th” to “14 dead on the 20th” → either `canonical_assertion` is rewritten (violates append-only, silent history loss) or a second claim lands in a new cluster with no `supersedes`/`retracts` edge → disagreement map shows a live vs dead fact as peers. Evidence: §5 relations are only `agrees|contradicts|refines`; no `superseded_by`, retraction flag, or claim version; §12 Q3 is asked and unanswered.

6. **P1 — Restatement licence check is specified backwards and unenforceable.** Failure: a copied sentence scores *high* similarity to the snippet and passes; a genuine short restatement scores *low* and is rejected (or the inverse threshold is used and copies ship). Either way §4’s “length cap plus similarity check” does not implement the band it needs (grounded ∧ not a reproduction). Evidence: §4 “length cap plus a similarity check against the stored snippet”; §10.5 “asserted, not proven.”

## ATTACKS
- Correctness: Happy path is “N2 produces rows.” Missing: extraction cursor/lease on items (two workers double-extract); denormalized `distinct_origins` races and goes stale; `asserted_about date` drops timezone and has no null policy for undated headlines; each claim `}o--||` one cluster so conjunctions and multi-event sentences are forced into a single bucket; W0 emits `intelligenceWiki.ts` frontmatter (`confidence`, `lastVerified`, `staleAfterDays`) before any extractor exists — fake confidence on an RSS reader; SimHash/MinHash is run on *LLM restatements* not snippets, so the clusterer fingerprints the extractor and misses rewritten wire copy (§10.2); “MOST CORROBORATED” / “4 vs 2” (§7.1–7.2) reintroduces majority-as-truth after §3 forbade it; W3 is L-size before the W1 20-claim manual gate, so the expensive slice is built on unmeasured error; no retention vs 1,445 items/poll (§10.4) — FTS and cluster tables grow without bound; `<=300 lines/file` has no split plan for the W3 clusterer+map.

- Security: No authz on “YOUR VERDICT” (IDOR / last-write-wins on a missing table); W5 inbound is unsanitized owner text into wiki pages (stored XSS) and into W6 (prompt injection); W4/Internet Archive and official connectors are SSRF + huge-fetch DoS with no size/type allowlist; W6 ships claim text (names, events) to Hermes/Codex/Claude — PII-to-LLM, and secrets for those providers are unmentioned; RSS/official/inbound collapse into one global knowledge layer with no tenant/owner scope on `entity`/`claim` (multi-tenant leak if SwanGuard is not a single-owner box); no rate limit on sync or extractor fan-out (1,445 × claim fan-out × N polls is a self-DoS); replay: re-enable connector re-ingests the world.

- Data-truth / schema drift: New singular `claim` / `entity` vs existing `comment_extracted_claims`, `comment_claim_fact_checks`, `influence_wiki_facts`, `company_trust_facts` — two claim systems, two verdicts, old tables remain 0; `outlet_id` FKs an `outlets` table that has no columns, while live data is `news_rss_sources` (39); `entity` as a table name is an ORM/SQL hazard and has no unique `(kind, canonical_name)`, no merge/split, no disambiguator; wiki generator still `WikiSourceModule = 'comment_intel' | 'influence_intel'` (`apps/api/src/intelligenceWiki.ts`) — W0 adds `news` but official/archival/inbound have no module; frontmatter `citations[]`/`wikilinks[]`/`staleAfterDays` have no column mapping from §5; `confidence` numeric with no 0–1 vs 0–100 constraint; N4 (official APIs / Federal Register “primary source” in §7.2) is in §1 and the flowchart but **absent from §9 slices**, so `official_connector_states` stays 0 and the primary-doc badge cannot light up; Pascal/snake is consistent inside §5 but plurality and naming diverge from live tables (`creator_item` vs `news_items` vs `entity`).

## HIGHEST RISK
Claim extraction against **headline+snippet+linkout only**, almost certainly via an LLM, is the entire product *and* a legal/PII landmine; a disagreement map on misread or copied sentences is worse than empty tables. Cheapest de-risk before any W1 code: ship N2, then **hand-extract** (no model) a 20-item sample from *real* stored snippets — record yield of atomic dated assertions, copy-vs-restate overlap, and whether two syndicated snippets SimHash together **on the snippet**. If yield is near-zero or syndication fails on snippets, stop; do not build W2/W3/W6.
