**House rules**

- styled-components only; no MUI.
- Victory only for new charts.
- Use the styled-components `css` helper for shared fragments.
- Every interactive hit area is at least 44×44px.
- Dark-first, keyboard accessible, reduced-motion aware.
- No component-local color literals; use verified centrally emitted tokens with approved fallbacks.
- No yoga/meditation terminology.
- No PII, raw media, provider credentials, or client identity in model input.
- Every code file ≤300 lines.
- User FKs reference `"Users"` with its actual key type.
- No `git add -A`; stage explicit owned paths.
- Commit format: `type(scope): description`.
- No production-branch push or deployment implied by this package.

**Feature bans**

- Do not rename GlowButton to Swan Forge.
- Do not add `sunny`, `cloud`, `winter`, or prototype `wing` themes.
- Do not import the dashboard-export prototype into production.
- Do not delete the motion assertion without replacing its performance/accessibility protection.
- Do not treat a missing device, failed query, or missing money policy as a numerical zero.
- Do not persist synthetic biometrics.
- Do not classify historical records as synthetic solely because values look unusual.
- Do not generate decorative charts from fake production data.
- Do not add depth to the equipment monolith before decomposition.
- Do not introduce another route authority beside the existing route tree.
- Do not expose video SDK configuration or placeholder implementation text to users.
- Do not record, snapshot, transcribe, or analyze video in this release.
- Do not turn connecting a device into permission to invoke Coach.
- Do not merge provider accounts by name/email.
- Do not sum overlapping device totals.
- Do not combine different HRV algorithms.
- Do not backfill or enforce 82%/15% from marketing copy.
- Do not execute payouts.

**Do not touch**

My Schedule behavior; unrelated body-map/nutrition/workout-generation code; Swan Forge content generation; archive query tooling; unrelated dirty files; historical review findings.

**Token deny rule**

Any consumed token not centrally emitted for every supported palette is banned. Proposed `--td-*` aliases are unusable until B-02 binds them. The current emitted set is unknown, so this package does not fabricate a list of existing bad tokens.
