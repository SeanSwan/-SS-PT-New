Section 4

Buttons — the GlowButton Family

Demonstrates: the mandatory Dual-Button Glow rule — blue background gets a purple glow; purple background gets a cyan glow — plus Rule 2 (≥44px touch targets) and :focus-visible rings (Wing Purple on blue, Ice Wing on purple). Tab through these samples to see the focus states. Any library suggestion that conflicts with Dual-Button Glow is rejected, not adapted.

Law 1 — Blue bg → Purple glow Midnight Sapphire / Royal Depth surface · Wing Purple #8B5CF6 outer glow + focus ring.
Law 2 — Purple bg → Cyan glow Wing Purple surface · Ice Wing #60C0F0 outer glow + focus ring.
min-height 44px · min-width 44px (Rule 2)

Primary = sapphire gradient with purple glow. Accent = purple with cyan glow (note the dark label for 4.5:1 contrast on the lighter purple surface). Luxury = Graphite with Gilded Fern border and label, gold glow on focus only. Ghost = electric border only. Danger = solid restrained red #E5484D with a near-black label and no glow — destruction is not celebrated. Disabled = Graphite, no glow, cursor: not-allowed.

Section 17

Motion — Runtime Quality + Accessibility

Demonstrates: every cinematic surface ships Full, Lean, and Still runtime-quality modes together. Reduced Motion is a separate accessibility override across all three. Motion always has a job — reveal, hierarchy, state, or reward. Decorative motion is noise and gets cut.

Full cinema
  • Licensed video, parallax, spatial accents
  • Framer layout tweens
  • Capable device and normal network
Lean cinema
  • Poster/encoded media instead of optional spatial work
  • CSS-only depth where safe
  • Low-power / save-data devices
Still
  • Authored semantic poster
  • No continuous runtime required
  • Copy, proof, navigation, and action preserved
Accessibility override — Reduced Motion
  • prefers-reduced-motion: reduce
  • Nonessential and scroll-linked motion removed
  • Applies across Full, Lean, and Still
loading-shimmer demo — animates ONLY under prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference

If your OS is set to reduce motion, the panel above is intentionally static — that's the fallback working, not a bug. GPU-safe properties only (transform, opacity); never animate layout.

Section 18

Dos & Don'ts

Demonstrates: the anti-template bans (design-system section B) plus the hard CLAUDE.md rules, side by side. When in doubt, the DON'T column is the list of things AI defaults to — resist all of it.

DOUse var(--token, #fallback) for every color, spacing, and blur value.
DON'THardcode raw hex in components — tokens with dark fallbacks only (Rule 6).
DOBlue button → purple glow · purple button → cyan glow (Dual-Button Glow).
DON'TCyan buttons, cyan glows on blue, or any library "taste" that bans purple glow — rejected, not adapted.
DOReserve Arctic Cyan #50A0F0 for charts and data visualization.
DON'TPut Arctic Cyan on buttons, focus rings, or glows — it is data-only.
DOCompose with weighted, asymmetric grids and left-aligned editorial moments.
DON'TCenter everything, or ship equal 4-up box grids as the default composition.
DOFeed charts from real workout logs; mark placeholders as gaps to replace.
DON'TShip fake metrics or decorative KPI rows with no media/story anchor.
DOUse styled-components with CSS Grid composition (and the css`` helper for shared fragments).
DON'TUse Material-UI, Tailwind classes, or Recharts in new work (Rules 1, 10).
DOKeep every interactive element ≥44px with a visible :focus-visible ring.
DON'THide actions behind hover, nest interactive elements, or shrink touch targets.
DOGate all animation behind prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference and animate transform/opacity only.
DON'TShip motion with no job, scroll-jack, or animate layout properties.
DOUse glass as a depth cue with electric or gold borders (C12 — three recipes only).
DON'TUse glassmorphism as a gimmick or invent a fourth panel recipe.
DORefer to clients by ID (C-1042) in any AI-adjacent surface — zero PII to LLMs (Rule 8).
DON'TUse the retired Galaxy-Swan theme — these tokens are dead: #0a0a1a #00FFFF #7851A9
