Recovered from frontend/src/components/Button/glowButton.jsx at commit 6108f5018 — 2025-03-17, the first time this button ever entered the repo. The colours are identical to the version I showed you before, so the palette was never the problem. What I had missed is the mechanism: a gradient-filled circle, pushed up 44px and rotating 360° every 2 seconds behind a near-black face, so light sweeps around the rim like it is travelling over polished metal. Plus a 32px blurred blob that follows your cursor, tinted the button's own colour. I had flattened all of it into one static diagonal bar. That is why it read cartoony.

The rotation below is a real animation — it is moving. The cursor-glow is parked at a fixed offset because an artboard has no live pointer; in the app it tracks pointermove exactly as it did originally.
