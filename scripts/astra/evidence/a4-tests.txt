3. THE FIXTURE IS A CALIBRATED INSTRUMENT, NOT A CORPUS
===============================================================================
`scripts/astra/fixtures/pairs-12.jsonl` — 12 domain-distinct claim pairs.

  expected bands: {"auto":1,"fresh":8,"merge-band":3}
  self-best: 9/12  (the other 3 have a genuinely better candidate elsewhere in the set)
  S per pair: PAIR-01 0.8406 · PAIR-02 0.5233 · PAIR-03 0.7741 · PAIR-04 0.5989
              PAIR-05 0.5025 · PAIR-06 0.5103 · PAIR-07 0.7399 · PAIR-08 0.0214
              PAIR-09 0.4732 · PAIR-10 0.1623 · PAIR-11 0.3744 · PAIR-12 0.0170
  live config: auto.S 0.82 · mergeBand.low 0.55

WHY THE BANDS ARE WHAT THEY ARE. The engine's real AUTO gate (`corroborate.mjs:104`) is
`S >= auto.S && O >= auto.O && margin >= auto.margin && tokens >= auto.minTokens`, where
`margin = best.S - secondBest.S`. MARGIN DEPENDS ON THE CANDIDATE SET, NOT ON THE PAIR — so
a fixture whose pairs all share a small vocabulary has every pair's best match be some
OTHER pair's `b`, and a pair of disjoint principles can be labelled `auto` because a
different candidate scored 0.84. That happened on the second attempt: the label described
the CORPUS, not the PAIR. Domain-distinct pairs fixed it. PAIR-01 sits +0.0206 above the
gate, and 4 pairs are within 0.05 of the 0.55 floor so a small floor move is visible.

The three "ambiguous" pairs are a genuine property of the set, not a defect, and the
fixture records `isSelfBest` per row so a reader can tell the difference.
